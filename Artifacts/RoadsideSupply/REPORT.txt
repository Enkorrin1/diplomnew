Rogue Drive: Survival - roadside supply upgrade, 2026-09-30

Playable additions in the first region of the single-player expedition:
1. Patrol stash, 1.4 km: search 10 seconds; 28 pistol rounds, 1 medkit, 4 repair bolts.
2. Medical reserve, 3.2 km: search 6 seconds; 2 medkits, 14 pistol rounds, 1 battery.
3. Repair reserve, 9.6 km: search 14 seconds; 8 repair bolts, 1 wrench, 7 pistol rounds.

E starts or cancels a search. Walking away, being downed or losing player control
cancels it. Pause freezes the timer. Searching alerts actual local encounter guards;
the storm continues to move. Open lids and signs communicate state and exact remaining
supplies. Full pockets do not consume rewards; claimed rewards cannot be claimed again.

Cache histories are saved with the station checkpoint, including partial collection.
Stable identities preserve the same cache through streamed world unload/reload.
New caches are hidden during network co-op until shared server-authoritative loot exists.

Existing scavenge fixes:
- Backpacks, cardboard boxes and tool cabinets retain unclaimed rewards with full pockets.
- Partial claims track each granted reward and cannot replenish consumed items on retry.
- Abandoned trunk reward rolls remain stable; a trunk is only marked looted after transfer.
- Failed transfers do not play a completion sound.
